# URLy privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-18

URLy does not collect, store, sell or transmit any personal data. It makes no network requests and contains no analytics or tracking.

## What URLy accesses

- **Tab URLs and titles**: read only when you copy, to build the text placed on your clipboard. They never leave your browser.
- **Text you paste or select**: scanned locally for links to open. It is not stored.
- **Clipboard**: written only when you copy. Read only when you choose "Open links from clipboard" (shortcut or menu), after you grant clipboard access, to find the links to open. The text is not stored.
- **Preferences**: your mode, format, template and options are saved in the browser's local extension storage and removed when you uninstall URLy.

## Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue in the project repository.
